🤖 Privacy Policy How Contract Bot may process information Privacy at a glance: Contract Bot may process Discord identifiers and contract information needed to provide its features. It does not intentionally collect Discord passwords or payment-card information. 1. Information We May Process Depending on how Contract Bot is configured and used, the service may process: • Discord User IDs, usernames, or display names • Discord Server (Guild) IDs • Channel and message identifiers needed for bot features • Contract text and related information entered by users • Contract status, such as pending, accepted, denied, or completed • Dates, timestamps, configuration settings, and technical information needed to operate the bot 2. How Information Is Used Information may be used to: • Create and manage contracts • Associate contracts with participating users • Track contract status and history • Create or manage contract tickets • Provide support, troubleshoot problems, and maintain the service • Prevent abuse and protect the service 3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect Contract Bot is not designed to intentionally collect Discord passwords, authentication credentials, or payment-card information. Users should not submit such information through the bot. 4. Data Sharing Contract Bot does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the bot, such as hosting, database, logging, or infrastructure providers, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. 5. Data Retention and Deletion Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Contract Bot's features, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or meet applicable obligations. Where supported, server administrators or users may request deletion of information stored by the bot. 6. Security Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information processed by Contract Bot. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. 7. Discord Contract Bot is used through Discord. Discord may independently collect and process information under its own privacy practices. Contract Bot does not control Discord's data practices. 8. Children's Privacy Contract Bot is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's age requirements and applicable law. Do not use Contract Bot to knowingly collect information from children in violation of applicable requirements. 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when Contract Bot's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. The current version published on this page will apply to future use of the service. 10. Contact For privacy questions or requests concerning Contract Bot, contact the Contract Bot development team through the official support channel associated with the bot.
